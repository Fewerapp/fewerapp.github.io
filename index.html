Fewer support

Fewer helps you clear duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and big videos from your iPhone, right on the device.

Need help? Email fewerapp.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within two business days.
Where do deleted photos go?

To the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. They stay there for 30 days, so you can recover anything by mistake. Storage space comes back once they leave Recently Deleted, or right away if you empty that album yourself.

What does Compress Videos do?

It saves a smaller 1080p copy of each video you pick, with the same date and location, then asks iOS to remove the original. The original goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days. Album placement isn't copied, so re-add the new copies to any albums you use. Compress is part of Fewer Plus.

I deleted a lot. Why didn't my iPhone storage go up?

Two reasons. Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so the space comes back once that album clears, or right away if you empty it in Photos. And if you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage", many full-size originals live in iCloud, so part of what you freed is iCloud space rather than iPhone space.

Why does iPhone ask me to confirm deleting?

iOS always asks before any app removes photos or videos. That's an Apple safety feature, and nothing is removed unless you tap Delete.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Fewer, tap Go unlimited, then Restore at the bottom of the screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Fewer and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, so cancel in Settings first. To skip the charge after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. The Lifetime plan is a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Fewer upload my photos?

No. Everything is analyzed on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
