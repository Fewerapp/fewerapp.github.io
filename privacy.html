Fewer

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version: Fewer does not collect, store or share any of your data. Your photos and videos are analyzed on your iPhone and never leave it.

Who we are

Fewer is an iPhone app made by Deven Renner, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. Fewer has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive data from the app.

Your photos and videos

Fewer asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos. All of this analysis happens on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to us, or shared with anyone.

When you choose to delete items, iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted items move to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days before iOS removes them. When you compress a video (a paid feature), Fewer saves a smaller copy to your library and then asks iOS to remove the original, which also goes to Recently Deleted.

You can change photo access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Fewer.

Information stored on your device

Fewer keeps a few settings in the app's own storage on your iPhone, such as which photos you chose to keep, your daily free-delete count, your streak and how much space you have freed. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple only tells the app whether you have an active purchase. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, any reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. No information is sent to us.

Children

Fewer is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact
